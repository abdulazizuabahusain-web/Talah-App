====================
PRODUCT CONTEXT
====================

Tal’ah is a Saudi-native, privacy-first curated social meetup app.

It supports:
- Women-only Tal’ahs
- Men-only Tal’ahs
- No mixed groups at launch
- No public profile browsing
- No swiping
- No pre-meetup chat
- Admin-controlled matching
- Small groups of 3–5 users
- Saudi Arabic-first tone with English support

Tal’ah is NOT:
- a dating app
- a social media app
- an open events marketplace

The onboarding should feel:
- light
- warm
- Saudi-native
- conversational
- not like a personality test
- not like a long survey

MATCHING / SCORING CHANGES
====================

Revised matching model:

Hard filters:
- same gender
- same city

Strong preference:
- same preferred meetup type

Compatibility signals:
- interests overlap
- lifeStage alignment
- social energy balance
- conversation style compatibility
- personality traits as supporting signal

Remove scoring logic for:
- planning
- atmosphere
- interaction
- openness
- boundary

Admin compatibility helper should calculate:
1. Gender compatibility
- mixed woman/man group is invalid

2. City compatibility
- different city group should show warning or be invalid

3. Meetup type alignment
- show whether selected users prefer same meetup type

4. Interest overlap
- show shared interests
- calculate overlap percentage

5. Life stage alignment
- show if users are same/similar life stage or mixed

6. Social energy balance
- calculate average socialEnergyScore
- ideal range: -1 to +1
- above +1: group may be too high-energy
- below -1: group may be too quiet/reserved
- within range: balanced social energy

7. Conversation compatibility
- calculate spread of conversationDepthScore
- if spread is high, show note
- otherwise show reasonably aligned

8. Personality support
- show shared or complementary personality chips

Overall compatibility score suggestion:
- 30% hard filters: gender + city
- 20% interests overlap
- 15% meetup type alignment
- 15% social energy balance
- 10% conversation compatibility
- 5% life stage alignment
- 5% personality traits support

Labels:
- Excellent fit: 85–100
- Good fit: 70–84
- Moderate fit: 55–69
- Weak fit: below 55
