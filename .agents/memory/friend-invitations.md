---
name: Friend invitations
description: Rules for email-invited friends joining a Tal'ah request.
---

Accepted invited friends bypass compatibility scoring, but must pass the same hard safety requirements as ordinary matches: same gender and city, neither account flagged or mutually blocked, and no conflicting active request or group. Invitations belong to a request rather than group membership until acceptance.

**Why:** A person named by the requester should be honored without weakening the product’s safety rules or reserving a seat for someone who has not agreed to join.

**How to apply:** When requests are finalized, include only accepted invitations and construct a four-to-five-person total group. Treat pending, declined, expired, and cancelled-request invitations as non-members. Keep invitation acceptance, request cancellation, and group finalization conditional and transactional so concurrent changes cannot create an accepted invitee outside the group.
